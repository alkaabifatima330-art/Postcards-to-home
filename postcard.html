Childhood
🌴 UAE
Message
To Baba — I still remember how you'd wake us all up early before the souq opened, the smell of cardamom rising from the kitchen before anything else. You'd stand at the stove in your white kandura, humming. I didn't know then that those mornings were something to hold on to. I think about them every Friday, wherever I am.
To
Baba
Al Qasimia, Sharjah
From
Nour
Dubai, Al Quoz
